# CS417-Dont-Stop-Driving
## Description:
Don't Stop Driving is a VR simulation-horror game that blends immersive driving mechanics with high-stakes puzzle management. Inspired by the "11 Miles" creepypasta and titles like Dredge, the game challenges you to maintain a vehicle - and your mind - against an escalating tide of surreal anomalies. As you navigate a cursed, endless highway, your car's dashboard is your only lifeline. You must toggle the radio, manage your lights, and control your speed to satisfy a shifting set of rules that govern the road. Every mile introduces new instructions, but every mistake fractures your sanity, inviting thick fog, distorted geometry, and a mysterious, hungry entity closer to your window. Follow the rules, manage the car, and whatever you do, don't stop driving.
